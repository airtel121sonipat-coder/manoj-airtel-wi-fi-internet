Built around study hours

What a student connection in Bhiwani has to get right

Online classes, recorded lectures and mock tests fail in the evening, not in the afternoon, which is when a whole lane is online together. So for a student the useful question is not only the top speed but whether the line stays steady for three or four hours and whether uploads, like submitting a test, finish without a retry.

  • One student, own room: the 40 Mbps Fiber plan is usually enough for classes and video lectures.
  • Shared room or PG with several devices: look at 100 Mbps so that one person's download does not stall another's class.
  • Wired Fiber first: a cable connection is generally steadier than wireless. AirFiber is the alternative where a cable cannot be laid.
  • Router placement: the free Wi-Fi router gives a stronger signal when it sits in the study room itself, not two walls away.
