40 Mbps vs 100 Mbps vs 300 Mbps: Which Speed Is Right for You?

Airtel Xstream Fiber in Sonipat comes in several speeds, and the fastest plan isn't always the right one for your home. Here's how to think about it based on actual usage, not just the number on the box.

40 Mbps — light, single-user homes

Good for one or two people browsing, YouTube, WhatsApp calls and basic streaming in HD. If nobody in the house is gaming or running multiple 4K streams at once, this is enough and keeps the monthly bill low.

100 Mbps — the most common choice

This is what most Sonipat households end up on. It comfortably handles 3–4 devices at once — a video call in one room, a 4K show in another, plus phones and laptops in the background — without buffering.

300 Mbps — families, gamers and multi-TV homes

Worth it if you have multiple TVs streaming simultaneously, someone gaming online, or several people on video calls for work at the same time. Also useful if you regularly upload or download large files.

Our honest recommendation

Don't pay for 300 Mbps if you're a single user who mostly browses and streams one device at a time — 100 Mbps will feel identical in daily use. But if buffering during peak evening hours is a regular complaint in your house, that's usually a sign it's time to step up.
