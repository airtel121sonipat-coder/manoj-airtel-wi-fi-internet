How to Get a New Airtel Fiber Connection in Sonipat

Getting Airtel Xstream Fiber set up at a new home or office in Sonipat is a simple 4-step process. Here's exactly how it works, start to finish.

Step 1: Check availability at your address

Fiber cabling has to physically reach your building. Send us your address on WhatsApp or call 9255820000 — we'll confirm within minutes whether Fiber is feasible, or whether AirFiber (wireless) is the better fit for your location.

Step 2: Pick a plan

Tell us how you'll use the connection — light browsing, work-from-home, a family that streams on multiple TVs, or a gaming setup — and we'll recommend a speed that actually matches, instead of overselling you a bigger plan than you need.

Step 3: Share your ID

A government photo ID (Aadhaar, voter ID, etc.) and a passport-size photo are usually all that's needed for verification.

Step 4: Installation

In most serviceable areas of Sonipat, Kundli, Rai, Murthal, Kharkhoda, Ganaur, Gohana and Barhi, installation is completed the same day, including the Wi-Fi router setup and a quick speed test before our technician leaves.

What if Fiber isn't available yet at my address?

Airtel AirFiber is a wireless alternative that delivers similarly fast speeds without needing a physical cable run — a good option for newer buildings or areas where fiber trenching hasn't reached yet.
