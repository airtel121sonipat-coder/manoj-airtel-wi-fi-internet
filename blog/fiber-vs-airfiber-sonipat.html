Airtel Fiber vs AirFiber: Which One Should You Choose in Sonipat?

If you're getting a new internet connection in Sonipat, Kundli, Rai, or nearby, you'll usually be choosing between two Airtel options: Xstream Fiber and AirFiber. Both are fast and reliable, but they suit different situations.

Choose Airtel Xstream Fiber if...

You own your home or plan to stay long-term, your building allows cable entry, and you want the highest possible speeds (up to 1 Gbps). Fiber is a physical cable run to your home, so it needs a one-time cable pull, but once installed it's extremely stable, even during peak evening hours.

Choose Airtel AirFiber if...

You're in a rented flat or PG and can't get permission for cable work, you live in a society where digging isn't allowed, or you want a connection that's quick and easy to move if you relocate. AirFiber is a compact wireless device — no cable digging, and speeds are close to fiber in most covered areas.

What about price?

Both start at similar entry prices. The real difference is installation — Fiber needs feasibility checked at your address, AirFiber is quicker to set up almost anywhere with coverage.
