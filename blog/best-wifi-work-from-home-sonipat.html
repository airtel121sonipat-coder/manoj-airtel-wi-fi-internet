Best Wi-Fi Plan for Work From Home in Sonipat

If your video calls freeze or your screen share lags, the problem is usually not your laptop — it's your upload speed and how many devices are sharing the connection at home.

How much speed do you actually need?

For one person working from home with video calls, 100 Mbps is comfortably enough. If two or more people in the house are on calls or streaming at the same time, 300 Mbps avoids slowdowns during peak hours.

Wired vs Wi-Fi for calls

Where possible, connect your work laptop to the router with a cable, or sit close to the router. Video calls are more sensitive to a weak Wi-Fi signal than regular browsing or streaming.

Router placement matters

Keep the router in an open, central spot — not inside a cupboard or behind a TV unit. Walls and metal furniture weaken the signal more than distance does.

Our recommendation

For most single work-from-home setups in Sonipat, our 100 Mbps Popular plan is enough. For shared households with multiple remote workers, we suggest stepping up to a higher-speed fiber plan.
